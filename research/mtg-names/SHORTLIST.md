# MTG naming shortlist — 2026-10-29

**All names below are actual MTG cards.** The list is exploratory, not approved
skill nomenclature. The links point to the original cards. The Japanese rules
notes are deliberately short paraphrases, not a reproduction of Oracle text.

**Selection order:** *appealing title → tone of the complete 14-skill roster →
possible assignments*. We are not translating the skill effects into English.
The MTG card's own mechanic has no veto power over assigning its name.

## A deliberately *provisional* 14-skill arrangement

This is **not an approved naming change**. The three chassis variants
share one card title as their base, so 12 unique titles cover 14 skills.

| Existing confirmed skill | Candidate | Why consider it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame Combat Proficiency — Light / Standard / Heavy | **True Conviction** (Light / Standard / Heavy) | 大胆な抽象名。弱点は機体との直接的なつながりの薄さ。 |
| Plant Your Feet | **Last Word** | 照準の正確さを説明せず、勝負を決めるイメージだけ残す。 |
| Keep Moving | **Escape Velocity** | 物理系SFの香り。 |
| On the Charge | **Through the Breach** | 小説的な突進感。 |
| Got Your Six | **Solidarity** | 実際の支援行為より、仲間への信頼を命名する。 |
| Hunker Down | **Withstand** | 力を受け止める静かな強さ。 |
| Go Dark | **Moment of Silence** | 強烈な雰囲気を持つ静かな名前。 |
| Fired Up | **Repercussion** | 感情ではなく因果の反動という印象。 |
| Keep Watch | **Second Sight** | 連続観測の技術名ではなく知覚のイメージ。 |
| Sweep the Sector | **See Beyond** | 独自の視野と探索の印象。 |
| On Your Own | **Against All Odds** | 単独行動そのものではなく不利を押し返す精神。 |
| Pick Your Ground | **Fortitude** | 地形スキルであることを説明しない、性格のある名称。 |

**Coherence check:** The combination is more varied than the earlier
imperative-heavy set. Risks: `Second Sight` and `See Beyond` lean on
similar sensory vocabulary; `Withstand` and `Fortitude` both suggest
defense; `True Conviction` might sound more like courage than chassis
proficiency. These are **criticisms**, not automatic rejection reasons.

## Deliberate exclusions

- `High Ground`: strong card name but a Star Wars meme association is
  already an unwanted distraction for this project.
- Proper MTG character/world names and cross-franchise titles: raw catalog
  includes them, shortlist intentionally avoids them.
- `Fieldcraft`, `Wingman`, etc. that are not verified exact MTG names
  were not quietly passed off as MTG cards.

Next human review should begin with the *whole roster*, not mechanically
matching a single skill to a card. Source effects are provided for context.
